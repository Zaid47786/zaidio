02 the short version

Curiosity turns
into action.

I don’t learn technology only by following tutorials. I learn by trying to build something that doesn’t exist yet — then investigating every problem that appears along the way.

That means experimenting with websites, web applications, databases, hosting environments, and the tools behind them. The bugs, deployment issues, and unexpected failures are part of the education.

“Understand the problem. Break it down. Solve it piece by piece.”
